Two ways to rank the tiny web. One of them goes surfing.

How does this work?
  1. Old way: count how many times your word appears on each page. Most words wins.
  2. Surfer way: a pretend surfer lands on a random page and makes 100,000 moves.
  3. On each move, 15% of the time the surfer gets bored and jumps to any page. Otherwise they click a random link.
  4. Pages the surfer visits often get a high score. Pages with many incoming links get visited a lot.
  5. So a page can say a word only once and still win, if the web keeps sending the surfer there.
